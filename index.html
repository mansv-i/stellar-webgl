Astrophysics & Engine Architecture

1. Lin-Shu Density Wave Theory Spiral arms are not rigid structures of stars rotating together (which causes the famous winding dilemma). They are semi-static gravitational compression waves through which stars pass—like a traffic jam on a galactic highway.
2. Dark Matter & Flat Rotation Curves Unlike our solar system where outer planets orbit slower ($v \propto r^{-1/2}$), real galactic discs maintain near-constant orbital speeds far out ($v \approx \text{const}$). This anomaly directly proved the existence of dark matter halos.
3. Stellar Demographics & Dust Lanes Galactic cores consist of older, lower-mass, yellowish stars ($K/M$ giants), whereas spiral arms are active stellar nurseries glowing blue with short-lived massive stars ($O/B$ spectral classes) accompanied by dark dust absorption lanes.
4. Zero-Dependency GPU Ray-Plane Math Every particle’s position, differential orbit, mouse gravity well, and shockwave propagation is evaluated purely in parallel within the WebGL2 vertex shader at 60 FPS without external libraries.
